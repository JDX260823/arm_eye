The deliverable is a Chinese, slide-by-slide speaking guide for the 10-slide compact defense deck.
Content is based on the latest deck and the user's rehearsal decisions: arm is the reliable control base;
arm_eye adds PC-camera MediaPipe gesture recognition; G is a recorded grasp trajectory with J4 closing
and J3 coordinated; U is J3 reverse rotation by 15 degrees; D is the reverse of U; R controls J1;
J2 is held at 78 degrees during grasping; GPIO13 long-press resets all joints to 90 degrees.
